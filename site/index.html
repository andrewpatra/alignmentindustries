Our Mission

Here at Alignment Industries, our mission is to serve the general public by persisting at the forefront of innovation. Since the beginning, our founder has upheld this mantra with his invention of the portable radio. Connecting people is our specialty, and we pride ourselves in how our products have played a part in the alignment of the world's hopes and dreams. We have been in service of our mission for about 40 years and hope to continue our pursuit for many more years to come.
